PROJECT MISSION

Zero Preventable Blood Stockouts

Empower blood banks, hospitals, and voluntary donors with advance shortage intelligence so critical trauma and surgical care never waits for emergency phone trees.

PROBLEM STATEMENT

Fragmented & Reactive Supply

Isolated hospital inventories miss seasonal donation dips, regional demand spikes, and 5-day platelet expiry windows—causing simultaneous shortages and wastage.

PROPOSED SOLUTION

Predictive Coordination Network

Centralizes multi-hospital supply signals, predicts blood-group deficits days ahead, and recommends optimal ABO/Rh inter-bank redistributions.

INNOVATION

Forecasting + FEFO Routing

Combines multi-horizon demand forecasting with First-Expired, First-Out (FEFO) compatibility matching across real city corridors.

SOCIAL IMPACT

Faster Trauma & Rural Care

Reduces search latency for rare groups (O-, AB-, B-) while cutting avoidable component expiry through proactive sharing.

FUTURE SCOPE

e-RaktKosh & Cold-Chain IoT

Direct HL7/FHIR hospital integration, refrigerated IoT temperature telemetry, and Scikit-learn / Prophet regional training pipelines.
